Help & keyboard shortcuts

Workflow

  1. Add content — drop images/SVGs, text, icons, shapes, or draw freehand. Each becomes a layer.
  2. Choreograph — pick an animation style per layer in Animate: hand-drawn outlines, chalk, scanner, spiral, typewriter…
  3. Direct the camera — add keyframes or hit Auto-focus layers for cinematic zooms.
  4. Arrange time — drag layer bars in the timeline; overlap to draw together.
  5. Or just write JSON — import a hand-authored JSON storyboard (Import JSON in the top bar / welcome screen) and get a full video automatically. Grab the ✨ sample on the welcome screen for the format.
  6. Scenes — chain boards with transitions; add title & outro cards.
  7. Export — WebM/MP4 video with optional voiceover & music, or a GIF/PNG.

Privacy

Everything runs locally in your browser. Projects auto-save to this device; nothing is uploaded.
